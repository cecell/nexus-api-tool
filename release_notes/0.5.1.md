Readable text from desc, changelog and file-note.

## New

- **--plain** on desc, changelog and file-note: readable text instead of the stored BBCode. Links become text (url), list items - item, headings and spoiler titles their own lines; images are dropped, videos become [video: url], byte-order marks and zero-width characters are removed, and runs of blank lines collapse to one. Bracketed text that isn't a BBCode tag ([WIP]) stays. Without --plain the output is unchanged.
